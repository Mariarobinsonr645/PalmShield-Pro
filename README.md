## 🛠️ Setup and Installation Guide

This section provides a more thorough look at the setup process, with additional tips to make sure everything works flawlessly. Let's dive deeper.

### System Requirements

To run PalmShield-Pro on your computer, you will need:

- A Windows computer (Windows 10 or Windows 11 is recommended).
- At least 2 GB of free hard drive space.
- A USB port and a compatible USB cable to connect your tablet.
- One of the supported Android tablets: Xiaomi Pad 7, OnePlus Pad, Lenovo Tab series, Google Pixel Tablet, or similar devices.
- Your tablet should have the latest Android operating system updates installed.

### Step-by-Step Setup

1.  **Download the Setup File** – Use the instructions in the Getting Started section above. If you already downloaded the file, skip to the next step.
2.  **Run the Installer** – Locate the downloaded `PalmShield-Pro-Setup.exe` file in your Downloads folder. Double-click it. If Windows shows a security prompt saying "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?" click "Yes."
3.  **Follow the Wizard** – The setup wizard will guide you. Choose the destination folder or leave it as the default. Click "Install" and wait for the progress bar to complete. This usually takes less than a minute.
4.  **Launch the Application** – Once installation is complete, you can either click "Launch Now" in the wizard or find PalmShield-Pro in your Start Menu. Open it.
5.  **Connect Your Tablet** – Use a good-quality USB cable. Plug one end into your tablet and the other into your computer. Unlock your tablet screen.
6.  **Enable USB Debugging** – On your tablet, you may see a popup asking to allow USB debugging. If you see it, tap "Allow" and check "Always allow from this computer." Then tap "OK." If you don't see this popup, don't worry—PalmShield-Pro will still work, but enabling USB debugging ensures the best connection. To manually enable it: on your tablet, go to Settings > About Tablet > Tap "Build Number" 7 times. Then go to Settings > Developer Options > Enable "USB Debugging."
7.  **Activate the Shield** – In the PalmShield-Pro window, you will see your tablet listed. Click the big button that says "Enable Shield." Wait for the status to change to "Active." Congratulations, your shield is now working.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

- **My tablet is not detected.** – Try a different USB cable. Some cables only charge and do not transfer data. Also make sure you have unlocked your tablet.
- **The shield is active but my palm still causes marks.** – Make sure your stylus pen is the one being used and not your finger. Some stylus pens need to be paired or connected first. Restart both the app and your tablet.
- **The application will not open on Windows.** – Right-click the application icon and choose "Run as administrator." This helps if Windows is blocking the program.
